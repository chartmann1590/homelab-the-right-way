About

What this site is

Practical homelab writing for people past the "install Proxmox, spin up Plex" stage.

The idea

Homelab the Right Way is a faceless brand: a YouTube channel, a set of ebooks and this blog, all about running Proxmox, OPNsense, Home Assistant and backups at home without the drama. The focus is the trap people walk into, the fix, and a checklist you can actually follow.

How the articles are made

Every article is based on the Homelab the Right Way ebooks. They are drafted with AI help from those books, then edited and published here with the matching YouTube video. Commands and settings come from the books; check them against your own setup before you run anything.
