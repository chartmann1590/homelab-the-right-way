EV Charge Tracker is live on Google Play: know what every mile really costs

EV Charge Tracker, a charging and efficiency logbook for EV drivers, is now on Google Play. Cost per kWh and per mile, home vs public split, live charging, service reminders, no account.

EV Charge Tracker, a new Android app from Hartmann Studios, is now live on Google Play. It is a charging and efficiency logbook built for EV drivers, starting from the kWh instead of the gallon. Log charges at home and on the road, see cost per kWh and per mile, and keep service reminders, all on your phone with no account.

What it does

  • Charge and fuel logs: kWh, cost, home or public, network and odometer. Gas and hybrid cars are supported too.
  • Home vs public split: how much of your charging is at home, and what the public network really costs.
  • Efficiency that matters: mi/kWh and cost per mile per vehicle, plus EV vs gas cost per mile.
  • Live charging: start a session, watch it on the lock screen, then stop and log the real final percentage.
  • Service reminders by mileage or by date: tires, cabin filter, inspection.
  • A home-screen widget with your average efficiency, last charge and a + Log button.
  • CSV export of all your logs.

Private by design

No account, no location permission, and no server. The optional backup file (Pro) goes only where you save it.

Free and Pro

Free covers one vehicle, unlimited logs, cost and efficiency statistics, reminders, live charging, CSV export and the widget, with a small banner ad. EV Pro (monthly or yearly subscription) removes ads and adds unlimited vehicles, the battery health trend, a fleet overview, and backup/restore to Drive or any storage provider.

In your language

Pick your language on first launch. The app translates its screens on your phone, and you can change it any time in Settings.
