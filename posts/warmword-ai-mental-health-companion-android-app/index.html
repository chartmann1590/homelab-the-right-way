WarmWord: a private on-device AI companion and journal

WarmWord is an Android AI companion and journal that runs its model on your device, works in your language and surfaces crisis resources fast.

Some thoughts are easier to say out loud than to keep circling. When anxious, racing or lonely thoughts get heavy, having somewhere to put them helps, even if it is not a person. WarmWord is an AI companion and journal for those moments, built so the conversation stays on your phone.

First, the important part, in the app's own spirit: WarmWord is not a substitute for professional care. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

What it does

  • A companion that listens without judgment. Chat whenever your thoughts feel too heavy to carry alone.
  • On-device AI. The model runs entirely on your device, so your conversations stay yours.
  • Your language, all the way through. Menus, buttons, screens and WarmWord's replies, translated by a small on-device model.
  • Journaling. A calm space to reflect and untangle the day.
  • Mood insights. Gentle insights that show patterns over time.
  • Crisis resources. Helpline and crisis resources surfaced in seconds when you need them.
  • Personal touches. Choose tones and set reminders.

Why on-device matters here

What you say to a mental health companion is about as personal as data gets. WarmWord's answer is to keep the AI on your phone. The chat model runs locally, and the translation model that puts the app into your language runs locally too, so, according to the listing, nothing leaves your device for either. If you have avoided apps like this because of where your words might end up, that design is the main reason to look at this one.

Using it in your own language

Pick a language during onboarding or later in settings. A small translation model on the phone then translates the whole app, including the companion's replies, not just the menus. You get the same experience in your language without an online translation service in the loop.

Chat, journal, notice

The pieces work best together:

  1. Chat when something is weighing on you right now and you want a response.
  2. Journal when you want to slow down and write the day out for yourself.
  3. Check your mood insights now and then to spot quiet patterns, like which days tend to be harder.

Reminders can nudge you to check in, and you can adjust the tone of the companion so it feels right to you.

When you need more than an app

WarmWord keeps crisis and helpline resources a few seconds away. If you are in danger or someone else is, use them, or call your local emergency services. A companion app can be a place to think. It cannot replace a professional, and it is not meant to.

Good to know

  • Price: free, with ads.
  • Premium: an upgrade to Premium unlocks unlimited conversations and deeper insights. The Play listing shows in-app purchases.
  • Category and rating: Health & Fitness, rated Everyone.
  • Disclaimer from the app: not a substitute for professional care. In an emergency, always contact your local emergency services.

Is it for you?

  • You want somewhere private to talk through anxious or lonely thoughts.
  • You care that the AI runs on your phone instead of a server.
  • You would like the whole app, replies included, in your own language.
  • You want a journal and mood patterns alongside the chat, with crisis resources close by.
