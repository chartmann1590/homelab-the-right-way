This is the running list behind our product Shorts and TikToks: tech that keeps going viral AND has kept selling well for years, filtered for a homelab, desk-setup and smart-home audience. Each one got its own short video with a fictional AI presenter (the presenter is AI-generated; we do not claim to have personally tested these). No prices or ratings here because they change weekly; check the listing.

1. TP-Link TL-SG105 5-port gigabit switch

The first upgrade almost every homelab makes: one cable from the router gives you four more full-speed gigabit ports for the server, NAS, Pi and desktop. Metal case, fanless, nothing to configure. See it on Amazon

2. SanDisk 2TB Extreme Portable SSD

A pocket solid-state drive with no moving parts, USB-C, rated for drops and splashes, fast enough that a full laptop or VM backup takes minutes. See it on Amazon

3. INIU 10,000 mAh 45W power bank

Jacket-pocket size, 45 W over USB-C (enough for a USB-C laptop, tablet or Steam Deck), a short built-in detachable cable plus a USB-A port, flight-safe capacity. See it on Amazon

4. Kasa Smart Plug Ultra Mini EP10 (2-pack)

A Wi-Fi plug with no hub and no subscription: hard-reboot a frozen mini PC or router from your phone, schedule desk lights, works with Alexa, Google Home and Home Assistant. See it on Amazon

5. Alex Tech 10 ft split cable sleeve

Braided split tubing you push the cables into: ten seconds to a clean bundle behind a desk or under a rack shelf, cut to length, reusable, keeps pets off the cords. See it on Amazon

6. KAMRUI AK1PLUS mini PC (Intel N5095, 16 GB)

A palm-size, low-power desktop that is a popular first Proxmox or Home Assistant box: dual HDMI, 16 GB RAM, an M.2 SSD you can swap, and it sips power on a shelf 24/7. See it on Amazon

7. TP-Link Archer AX21 AX1800 Wi-Fi 6 router

The budget Wi-Fi 6 router that shows up in every "what router should I buy" thread: dual-band, gigabit ports, OneMesh, and it runs as a plain access point behind an OPNsense box. See it on Amazon

8. Anker 8-in-1 USB-C docking station

One cable from the laptop to two monitors, Ethernet, USB-A ports and power delivery; the desk dock people keep recommending for hybrid work setups. See it on Amazon

9. Govee 16.4 ft RGBIC LED strip

The strip behind half the desk and rack photos online: segment colours, app and voice control, music sync, and it is the Govee line Home Assistant users pick. See it on Amazon

10. Anker 2100J surge-protector power strip

A flat-plug surge protector with a 2100-joule rating, multiple outlets and USB ports: the strip to put under a desk full of chargers and small boxes. See it on Amazon

How we pick

A product makes this list when it sits near the top of its Amazon Best Sellers category, has a long record of reviews over several years, and is the kind of thing people in the homelab and desk-setup corners of YouTube, TikTok and Reddit keep making videos about. The list is refreshed as new videos go up; the newest picks are at the top of the channel's Shorts.
