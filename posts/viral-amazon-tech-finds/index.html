This is the running list behind our product Shorts and TikToks: tech that keeps going viral AND has kept selling well for years, filtered for a homelab, desk-setup and smart-home audience. Each one got its own short video with a fictional AI presenter (the presenter is AI-generated; we do not claim to have personally tested these). No prices or ratings here because they change weekly; check the listing.

1. TP-Link TL-SG105 5-port gigabit switch

The first upgrade almost every homelab makes: one cable from the router gives you four more full-speed gigabit ports for the server, NAS, Pi and desktop. Metal case, fanless, nothing to configure. See it on Amazon

2. SanDisk 2TB Extreme Portable SSD

A pocket solid-state drive with no moving parts, USB-C, rated for drops and splashes, fast enough that a full laptop or VM backup takes minutes. See it on Amazon

3. INIU 10,000 mAh 45W power bank

Jacket-pocket size, 45 W over USB-C (enough for a USB-C laptop, tablet or Steam Deck), a short built-in detachable cable plus a USB-A port, flight-safe capacity. See it on Amazon

4. Kasa Smart Plug Ultra Mini EP10 (2-pack)

A Wi-Fi plug with no hub and no subscription: hard-reboot a frozen mini PC or router from your phone, schedule desk lights, works with Alexa, Google Home and Home Assistant. See it on Amazon

5. Alex Tech 10 ft split cable sleeve

Braided split tubing you push the cables into: ten seconds to a clean bundle behind a desk or under a rack shelf, cut to length, reusable, keeps pets off the cords. See it on Amazon

6. KAMRUI AK1PLUS mini PC (Intel N5095, 16 GB)

A palm-size, low-power desktop that is a popular first Proxmox or Home Assistant box: dual HDMI, 16 GB RAM, an M.2 SSD you can swap, and it sips power on a shelf 24/7. See it on Amazon

7. TP-Link Archer AX21 AX1800 Wi-Fi 6 router

The budget Wi-Fi 6 router that shows up in every "what router should I buy" thread: dual-band, gigabit ports, OneMesh, and it runs as a plain access point behind an OPNsense box. See it on Amazon

8. Anker 8-in-1 USB-C docking station

One cable from the laptop to two monitors, Ethernet, USB-A ports and power delivery; the desk dock people keep recommending for hybrid work setups. See it on Amazon

9. Govee 16.4 ft RGBIC LED strip

The strip behind half the desk and rack photos online: segment colours, app and voice control, music sync, and it is the Govee line Home Assistant users pick. See it on Amazon

10. Anker 2100J surge-protector power strip

A flat-plug surge protector with a 2100-joule rating, multiple outlets and USB ports: the strip to put under a desk full of chargers and small boxes. See it on Amazon

11. Amazon Smart Plug

The plug that makes any lamp, fan or coffee maker voice-controlled through Alexa, with no hub to buy: it sets up from the Alexa app in minutes, runs timers, schedules and routines, and reconnects on its own after a power or Wi-Fi outage. The compact shape leaves the second outlet free. See it on Amazon

12. Seagate 2TB portable hard drive

A plug-and-play USB 3.0 drive for Windows or Mac with 2 TB of space and no software to install: connect it, drag and drop, and the backup is done. It ships with an 18-inch USB 3.0 cable and is the drive people grab for a cheap offsite copy of their homelab backups. See it on Amazon

13. Syntech USB-C to USB adapter (2-pack)

Two small zinc-alloy adapters that turn a USB-C or Thunderbolt port into a USB 3.0 Type-A port, rated for 10,000 plug cycles, plug and play with no driver. They work with modern MacBooks, iPhones with USB-C and most USB-C laptops, tablets and phones, and come in a small foam case. See it on Amazon

14. WD 2TB Elements portable hard drive

Western Digital's plug-and-play portable drive with 2 TB in a small, light enclosure over SuperSpeed USB 3.2 Gen 1 (5 Gbps), for PC and Mac, with a USB 3.0 cable in the box. The Elements line goes up to 6 TB if you want a bigger one for the same job. See it on Amazon

15. Jadaol 50 ft flat Cat6 Ethernet cable with clips

A flat, white 50-foot Cat6 patch cable rated to 250 MHz and 10 Gbps, with gold-plated RJ45 ends and bare-copper conductors, that runs along baseboards, around corners or under a carpet and comes with clips to pin it in place. The cable for the room the Wi-Fi never quite reaches. See it on Amazon

16. SanDisk 128GB Ultra Dual Drive (USB-C and USB-A)

A 128 GB flash drive with two retractable connectors, a reversible USB-C plug on one end and a standard USB-A plug on the other, so the same stick moves files between a phone, a tablet and a computer without an adapter. USB 3.1 Gen 1 with read speeds up to 150 MB/s, and SanDisk's Memory Zone app for Android manages and backs up phone content onto it. See it on Amazon

17. QINLIANF 5-outlet wall extender with 4 USB ports

A surge-protecting outlet extender that screws onto a standard duplex wall outlet: five AC outlets on three sides spaced 2.1 inches apart, plus three USB-A ports and one USB-C port with automatic charging detection, and 1,680 joules of surge protection with a "Protected" indicator light. ETL certified; it fits duplex outlets only, not GFCI. See it on Amazon

18. Logitech MK270 wireless keyboard and mouse combo

A full-size wireless keyboard with number pad, F-keys and eight multimedia hotkeys, paired with a compact ambidextrous mouse over one USB receiver with a range up to 33 feet. Spill-resistant design, adjustable tilt legs, and Logitech rates the batteries at 36 months for the keyboard and 12 months for the mouse, with on/off switches on both. See it on Amazon

19. Miady 2-pack 10,000 mAh slim power banks

Two slim 10,000 mAh power banks (one black, one white) with two USB-A ports and a bidirectional USB-C port, so each can charge up to three devices at once and recharges through the same USB-C port. Each measures about 5.5 by 2.6 by 0.6 inches and weighs about 7.7 ounces; two USB-C cables are in the box. See it on Amazon

20. Anker USB-C to USB-C cable, 60W, 6 ft (2-pack)

Two 6-foot braided-nylon USB-C to USB-C cables rated for 60 W Power Delivery charging and 480 Mbps data, with a core Anker rates for 5,000 bends and a hook-and-loop tie on each. USB-C only: no Lightning and no video output, which is exactly the cable you want for laptops, mini PCs, phones and tablets around the desk. See it on Amazon

21. HANYCONY 8-outlet surge protector power strip with 4 USB

A compact power strip with eight AC outlets across three sides, spaced 2.2 inches apart so bulky adapters do not block each other, plus two USB-A and two USB-C charging ports (5 V). It has a 5-foot braided cord with a flat right-angle plug, 1,080 joules of surge protection, a lit on/off switch with an integrated circuit breaker, and two keyhole slots for wall mounting. ETL and RoHS certified, rated 1875 W / 15 A. See it on Amazon

22. UGREEN Cat 8 braided Ethernet cable, 6 ft

A 6-foot Cat 8 patch cable rated for 40 Gbps and 2,000 MHz, built with four foil-shielded twisted pairs (F/FTP), pure copper 26 AWG conductors and gold-plated RJ45 plugs inside a cotton braided jacket UGREEN rates for 10,000 bends. PoE is supported and it is backward compatible with Cat 7, Cat 6 and Cat 5e gear. Most home equipment runs at 1 to 10 Gbps, so the shielding and durability are the practical gains, not the headline speed. See it on Amazon

23. havit HV-F2056 laptop cooling pad

A slim USB-powered cooling pad for 15.6 to 17-inch laptops with three quiet fans under a metal mesh deck, two adjustable height settings so it doubles as a stand, a power switch and an extra USB port. It measures about 15 by 11 by 1.2 inches. The included cable is USB-A, so a USB-C-only laptop needs a small adapter. See it on Amazon

24. Delamu cord hider raceway kit, 157 in

A wall cable raceway kit with 157 inches (13.1 feet) of pre-sectioned channel, 0.95 inches wide by 0.55 inches tall, that holds two to four cables under a snap-on, snap-off cover. It mounts with adhesive or screws, cuts to length, can be painted to match the wall, and ships with matching connectors and corner pieces; the PVC carries a UL94 V-0 flame-retardancy rating. The usual fix for the cables hanging under a wall-mounted TV. See it on Amazon

25. Logitech G305 Lightspeed wireless gaming mouse

A compact wireless gaming mouse with Logitech's HERO optical sensor (up to 12,000 DPI, 400 IPS) and LIGHTSPEED wireless at a 1 ms report rate, weighing 3.4 ounces. It runs about 250 hours on a single AA battery, or up to nine months in Endurance mode set through Logitech G HUB, has six buttons on an ambidextrous shape, and the nano receiver stores inside the mouse. See it on Amazon

26. NETGEAR GS305 5-port Gigabit Ethernet switch

A five-port unmanaged switch with 1 Gbps on every port, auto-negotiation that picks the right speed for each device, and support for the Cat5e or Cat6 cables you already have. It is plug-and-play with no software or setup, runs silent with no fan, uses IEEE 802.3az Energy Efficient Ethernet to cut power draw, and its compact metal case sits on a desk or mounts on a wall. The quick fix when the router runs out of ports. See it on Amazon

27. UGREEN 5-in-1 USB-C hub with 4K HDMI and 100W PD

A pocket USB-C adapter that adds a 4K HDMI output, a USB-C Power Delivery input that passes up to 100W through to the laptop, one 5 Gbps USB-A data port and two 480 Mbps USB-A ports. Handy for thin laptops with only one or two USB-C ports: a monitor, a charger, a keyboard, a mouse and a flash drive all hang off a single port. See it on Amazon

28. INIU 20,000 mAh pocket power bank with built-in cable

A compact 20,000 mAh power bank (about 11 ounces) that INIU rates for roughly four full phone charges, with 22.5W fast charging, a built-in USB-C cable that doubles as a woven nylon lanyard, extra ports for other cables, and a small built-in flashlight. Its capacity is within the airline carry-on limit, and INIU backs it with a 3-year replacement warranty. See it on Amazon

29. Tapo C100 1080p indoor Wi-Fi camera

TP-Link's small plug-in indoor camera records 1080p Full HD with 850 nm infrared night vision up to 40 feet, person and motion detection with custom activity zones, baby-crying detection, and two-way audio with a built-in siren. Privacy mode pauses recording and privacy zones block out parts of the room. Video saves to a microSD card up to 512 GB or to the optional Tapo Care cloud. See it on Amazon

30. BESIGN LS03 aluminum laptop stand

A detachable aluminum laptop riser for 10 to 15.6-inch laptops that lifts the screen about 6 inches toward eye level, holds up to 8.8 lbs, and grips the laptop on rubber pads so it doesn't slide or scratch. The open frame lets air flow under the laptop, it assembles in three steps, and it comes apart flat for a bag or a drawer. See it on Amazon

31. Govee H6008 smart color light bulbs, 4-pack

Four A19 Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LED bulbs on a standard E26 base, each putting out 800 lumens at 9 watts, with 16 million colors and 54 preset scenes. They take voice commands through Alexa and Google Assistant, run on timers and Sunrise and Sunset schedules from the Govee Home app, group with other Govee devices, and can change color with music picked up by your phone's microphone. They need a 2.4 GHz network and are not meant for use with smart wall switches. See it on Amazon

32. Samsung BAR Plus 128 GB USB 3.1 flash drive

A 128 GB USB-A flash drive in a metal body with a built-in keyring loop. Samsung rates it waterproof, shock-proof, temperature-proof, magnet-proof and X-ray-proof, backs it with a 5-year limited warranty, and says a 3 GB 4K video moves to a PC in about 10 seconds. A sturdy stick for a bootable installer or an offline copy of your configs. See it on Amazon

33. Tapo C210 2K pan/tilt indoor camera

TP-Link's indoor camera records 2K video and pans 360 degrees and tilts 114 degrees, so one camera can sweep a whole room. It sends alerts for motion, people and a crying baby, has two-way audio with a built-in siren and night vision up to 30 feet, and records to a microSD card up to 512 GB with no subscription, or to the optional Tapo Care cloud. It needs 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and works with Alexa and Google Assistant displays. See it on Amazon

34. TP-Link UB500 Bluetooth 5.4 USB adapter

A nano-sized USB dongle that adds Bluetooth 5.4 to a Windows PC, backward compatible with older Bluetooth versions and supporting both EDR and Bluetooth Low Energy for headsets, controllers, phones and printers. It is plug and play on Windows 10 and 11 (Windows 7 needs a free driver from TP-Link) and does not support macOS, Linux or TVs. TP-Link's tip: turn off any built-in Bluetooth in Device Manager first to avoid driver conflicts. See it on Amazon

35. D-Line 6 ft floor cord cover

A flexible PVC floor cable protector, 2.4 inches wide and 0.44 inches tall, that covers up to two cables of about 0.31 inches across in its inner channel. It ships coiled but lies flat, cuts to length with a utility knife or heavy-duty scissors, and its ribbed non-slip base and split back mean no tape is needed. Made for the cord that runs across a walkway to the desk or the rack. See it on Amazon

36. Anker Zolo 10,000 mAh 30W power bank

A pocket power bank, 4.32 x 2.58 x 0.98 inches, with a 5.4-inch USB-C cable built in that Anker says is tested past 10,000 bends. It charges devices at up to 30W and refills itself at up to 20W, and Anker rates it at about two full charges for an iPhone 15. Handy for keeping a phone alive during a long rack session or a power cut. See it on Amazon

37. Nettbe reusable hook-and-loop cable ties, 60-pack

Sixty 6-inch nylon hook-and-loop straps that wrap bundles up to about 40 mm across and peel off again when you re-cable. Unlike zip ties they never need cutting, so the same strap can move from the desk to the rack to the garage. The cheapest way to tame the mess behind a switch or a TV. See it on Amazon

38. Kingston NV3 1 TB M.2 NVMe SSD

A single-sided M.2 2280 drive on PCIe 4.0 x4, rated by Kingston at up to 6,000 MB/s read and 4,000 MB/s write. It comes with Acronis cloning software for moving an existing system over, and a 5-year limited warranty. A fast boot or VM drive for a mini PC, laptop or Proxmox node. See it on Amazon

39. TP-Link Deco X55 AX3000 Wi-Fi 6 mesh, 3-pack

A Wi-Fi 6 mesh system that TP-Link rates for up to 6,500 square feet and up to 150 devices across the three units. Each unit has three gigabit Ethernet ports and supports wired Ethernet backhaul, any unit can act as the router, and setup runs through the Deco app. Basic network security, QoS and parental controls are included at no cost. See it on Amazon

40. Seagate IronWolf 8 TB NAS hard drive

A 3.5-inch SATA 6 Gb/s drive built for NAS boxes of up to 8 bays, with up to 256 MB of cache and a 1 million hour MTBF rating. IronWolf Health Management lets a supported NAS watch the drive's health, and it carries a 3-year limited warranty plus 3 years of Rescue data recovery service. A common pick for a home media or backup array. See it on Amazon

41. Samsung 990 PRO 2 TB M.2 NVMe SSD

A 2 TB M.2 2280 drive on PCIe 4.0 that Samsung rates at up to 7,450 MB/s sequential read and up to 6,900 MB/s sequential write. It sits straight on the motherboard with no cables, and Samsung's Magician software handles firmware updates and drive health. A roomy boot, game or VM drive for a desktop, workstation or Proxmox node. See it on Amazon

42. Anker 25,000 mAh 165W laptop power bank

A 25,000 mAh power bank with three 100W-capable USB-C ports and 165W of total output, plus a USB-A port, so it can charge up to four devices at once. Two USB-C cables are built in, one retractable up to 2.3 feet and one short cable that doubles as the carry strap, and Anker notes it stays inside the 100Wh airline carry-on limit. Enough to keep a laptop going through a long day away from an outlet. See it on Amazon

43. Samsung T7 1 TB portable SSD

A pocket-sized USB 3.2 Gen 2 SSD that Samsung rates at up to 1,050 MB/s read and 1,000 MB/s write. The aluminum body is rated for drops of up to 6 feet, it works with PCs, Macs and phones, and it can record 4K 60fps video straight from a supported camera or phone. A quick way to carry backups, ISOs or project files between machines. See it on Amazon

44. Govee RGBIC floor lamp Basic

A 53.7-inch corner floor lamp with up to 1,000 lumens and RGBIC segments, so different parts of the lamp can show different colors at the same time. It is controlled from the Govee Home app or by voice through Alexa or Google Assistant, it can sync its colors to music, and it connects over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only. A popular bit of bias lighting behind a desk or a rack corner. See it on Amazon

45. TP-Link UE306 USB 3.0 to gigabit Ethernet adapter

A small USB 3.0 Type-A to RJ45 adapter that adds a 10/100/1000 Mbps wired port to a laptop, tablet or Nintendo Switch, with no external power needed. TP-Link says it needs no driver on Windows 11/10, Linux or the Switch, and the USB plug folds for travel. Handy for a laptop with no Ethernet port, or for wiring into a switch when Wi-Fi is the problem. See it on Amazon

46. Airlonv LED desk lamp with clamp

A clamp-on desk lamp with a 17-inch wide light bar on a 360-degree flexible gooseneck. Color temperature runs from 2700K warm to 6500K cool with stepless dimming from 1% to 100%, the body is aluminum and ABS, and a 5V/3A power adapter is in the box. Airlonv sizes it for a work height of about 31 inches, so it reaches over monitors up to 30 inches. See it on Amazon

47. Anker USB-C to Ethernet adapter

A small Anker PowerExpand adapter that turns a USB-C 3.0 port into a wired gigabit Ethernet port. It is just over half an inch thick, about as heavy as a AA battery, with an aluminum shell and a braided-nylon cable, and Anker covers it with an 18-month warranty. The quick fix for a thin laptop with no Ethernet port when Wi-Fi is the problem. See it on Amazon

48. OPNICE 2-tier monitor stand and desk organizer

A metal monitor riser with two tiers, a pull-out drawer and two hanging pen holders, so the screen sits at a more comfortable height and the small desk clutter goes underneath. It comes with four height-adjustable non-slip pads to protect the desk, and OPNICE offers a 100-day return policy. A cheap way to free up desk space under a monitor or laptop. See it on Amazon

49. Anker 140W 4-port GaN charger with display

A GaN wall charger with four ports, two of them USB-C ports that each go up to 140W (PD 3.1), and a color screen that shows charging status, temperature and per-port details. Anker rates it at 50% on a 15-inch MacBook Air in about 30 minutes, it measures 2.72 x 2.72 x 1.42 inches, and a 5 ft 240W USB-C cable is included. One brick for a laptop, phone and accessories at once. See it on Amazon

50. Apple Mac mini (2024) with M4

A five-by-five-inch desktop built on Apple's M4 chip with a 10-core CPU and 10-core GPU, in the 16 GB unified memory and 256 GB SSD configuration. The back has Thunderbolt, HDMI and gigabit Ethernet, and for the first time the front has USB-C ports and a headphone jack. Small and quiet enough to sit beside a monitor, or on a shelf as an always-on home server. See it on Amazon
