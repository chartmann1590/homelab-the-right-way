Gig Mileage Tracker is live on Google Play: every business mile, counted

Gig Mileage Tracker, a mileage and earnings log for Uber, Lyft, DoorDash and Instacart drivers, is now on Google Play. Drive detection, one-tap classification, IRS standard rate deductions.

Gig Mileage Tracker, a new Android app from Hartmann Studios, is now live on Google Play. It is a mileage and earnings log built for gig drivers: Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart and more. Drives are detected on your phone, classified with one tap, and turned into a deduction at the IRS standard rate.

Free

  • Unlimited manual trips.
  • Drive detection with tap-to-confirm. Every detected drive waits for one tap, with no monthly limit. If GPS missed a drive's distance, type the miles in.
  • Fully automatic logging for 10 drives a month. After that, new drives ask you to confirm.
  • One-tap business or personal classification.
  • Tolls and parking expenses.
  • CSV export of trips, earnings and expenses.
  • Quarterly tax estimates on the Form 1040-ES periods, plus per-platform earnings, business miles and $/mile.
  • A home-screen widget with this week's deductible miles.
  • Ads on list screens.

Mileage Pro (monthly or yearly subscription)

  • No ads, and unlimited fully automatic trip logging.
  • Earnings CSV import: Uber, Lyft and DoorDash exports, or any spreadsheet where you match the date and amount columns. You preview before saving, and duplicates are skipped.
  • PDF mileage and deduction report for any period (this month, last month, year to date, last year), shared from your phone.
  • An accountant-ready annual PDF: totals, quarterly payment periods, platform breakdown, monthly miles and the full trip log with the IRS rate applied per trip date.

In your language

Pick your language on first launch or any time in Settings. The app translates its screens on your phone.
