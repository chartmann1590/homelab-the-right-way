Pawdub: your dog finally has subtitles (and yes, it's a joke app that really listens)

Pawdub puts funny movie subtitles on your dog or cat when it actually barks or meows. Real on-device sound and scene detection, 8 personas, 59 languages, no account and nothing uploaded. It is a joke app and says so. Free on Google Play.

Your dog barks at the front door. A subtitle fades in under him: "THE MAILMAN HAS RETURNED. I WILL HANDLE THIS." That is the whole idea behind Pawdub, a new Android app from Hartmann Studios. It is for fun, and it says so on the first screen: no app can translate animals, and Pawdub never claims to. What it does do is listen and look for real, then pick a line that fits.

What actually happens

Point the camera at your dog or cat. The app listens for a bark, whine, growl, howl, meow, purr or hiss, detected on your phone. When it hears one, a movie-style subtitle appears. No sound, no subtitle. It also looks at the scene (a door, a food bowl, the couch, a toy, a bed), so the joke matches the moment: a cat at the window gets a different line than a cat at an empty bowl.

The lines themselves are jokes written for each persona. They are not a reading of what your pet means, and the app gives no advice about your pet's health, mood, training or behaviour. If you are worried about your pet, talk to a vet.

Personas

Eight of them: Drama Queen, Golden Retriever Energy, Grumpy Old Man, Royal Highness, Tiny Gremlin, Food Critic, Conspiracy Theorist and Noir Detective. Each one has its own voice and its own set of lines, so the same bark becomes a very different movie depending on who is "talking".

59 languages, offline

Subtitles come in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Arabic and 50 more. Each language is downloaded from Google once and then works offline. Pro adds bilingual subtitles.

Clips you already have

Open a video from your gallery and Pawdub adds subtitles to the moments where your pet makes a sound. Styles: Cinema and Meme are free; Pro adds Bubble (a speech bubble that follows your pet) and Text message.

The rest

  • Pet voices: hear the line read aloud with your phone's text-to-speech.
  • Pet profiles for every dog and cat in the house.
  • Bark diary: a log of what your pet "said" today.
  • Stickers and poster templates made from your clips.
  • Export to your gallery and share anywhere.

Free and Pro

Free: unlimited live dubbing, a few exports a day with a small Pawdub tag, 3 personas, your language plus one more, the Cinema and Meme styles, and a starter sticker pack. Ads appear in the free version, never on the camera screen.

Pro: every persona, all languages, every style, all voices, longer gallery clips, write your own lines, unlimited exports without the tag, and no ads. It is sold as a subscription with a free trial for new subscribers, or as a one-time lifetime unlock; prices are shown in Google Play.

Privacy

Everything runs on your phone with on-device AI. The microphone and camera are used only while the camera screen is open, audio and video are processed on the device and never uploaded, and there is no account. Delete a clip from the library, or everything from Settings > Delete all data. The privacy policy is at chartmann1590.github.io/pawdub-site/privacy.

Pawdub is free on Google Play (the button below). The Pawdub site has more screenshots and the promo video.
