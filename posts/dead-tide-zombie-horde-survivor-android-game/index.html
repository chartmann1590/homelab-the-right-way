Dead Tide: a zombie horde survivor for Android, coming soon to Google Play

Survive the endless undead horde.

Dead Tide is a new game from Hartmann Studios: a top-down zombie horde survivor for Android phones. It is coming soon to Google Play.

How a run works

Your weapons fire on their own. You steer with one thumb and dash out of trouble, and the horde keeps coming from every side: shamblers, brutes and worse.

Every level-up offers three upgrades. Pick damage, speed or a new weapon and stack them into a build that can hold the line. Runs are short and frantic, so it fits a coffee break.

The arsenal

The weapons in the game include the Rocket Launcher, Combat Shotgun, Rail Sniper, Plasma Bolt, Storm Drone, Frost Nova, Chain Lightning, Flame Aura, Starfall and Meteor Strike.

Rescue your squad

Three survivors are out there: Sarge, Ghost and Sparks. Find their beacons, pull them out, and each one joins the fight.

Four chapters, four bosses

  • Cursed Graveyard
  • Crimson Catacombs
  • Frozen Desolation
  • Toxic Quarantine

Each chapter ends with its own boss.

Progress that sticks

Between runs, spend the gold you salvage on permanent armory talents. Every run makes the next one a little easier.

Free to play

Dead Tide is free with ads. There is an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase and optional gold packs. No account is needed, and your progress is saved on your phone.

Get it

Dead Tide is coming soon to Google Play. The game's site will link straight to the store the moment it is live: https://chartmann1590.github.io/dead-tide-site/.
